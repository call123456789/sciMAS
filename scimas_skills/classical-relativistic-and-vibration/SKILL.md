# Classical Relativistic And Vibration

Use this skill for oscillator dynamics (natural frequency, damping → Q, driven steady-state amplitude), Lagrangian / Hamiltonian construction, generalized momenta, and special-relativistic kinematics / dynamics.

For vibration, distinguish natural frequency from resonance frequency. For relativity, report beta = v/c and gamma side-by-side, and treat γ → ∞ as the speed-of-light limit.
