# Waves Doppler

Use this skill for classical Doppler shift (stationary-medium) and medical ultrasound Doppler blood-velocity estimation.

Always state the wave speed (sound in air ≈ 343 m/s, in tissue ≈ 1540 m/s) and report approach vs. recede cases separately.
